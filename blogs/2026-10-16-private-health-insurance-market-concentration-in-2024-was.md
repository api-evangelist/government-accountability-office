What GAO Found Since 2014, GAO has reported that enrollment in private health insurance plans has been concentrated, meaning a small number of issuers, such as health insurers, enrolled most of the people in a given market. Specifically, GAO considers a market concentrated in a state if three or fewer issuers hold at least 80 percent of the market share of enrollment.
