What GAO Found The Drug Enforcement Administration (DEA) and U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) have entered into longstanding formal agreements to enhance their collaboration on counternarcotics investigations, including a June 2009 interagency cooperation agreement and a January 2021 joint letter. These agreements describe how DEA and ICE’s Homeland Security Investigations (HSI) are to coordinate on investigations.
