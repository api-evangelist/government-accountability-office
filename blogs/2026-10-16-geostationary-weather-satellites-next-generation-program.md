What GAO Found The National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA) initially planned for the Geostationary Extended Observations (GeoXO) program to be a series of six satellites with a projected lifecycle cost of $19.6 billion. In August 2025, Commerce directed the GeoXO program office to implement a major restructuring to reduce lifecycle costs, and in April 2026 Commerce approved the program’s new baseline.
