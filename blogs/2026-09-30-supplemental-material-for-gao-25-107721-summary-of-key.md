This is a supplement to GAO’s 2025 revision of the Standards for Internal Control in the Federal Government, known as the Green Book, GAO-25-107721. The 2025 revision contained changes from, and supersedes, the 2014 revision of the Green Book, GAO-14-704G. The purpose of this supplement is to present the key changes to the 2025 revision of the Green Book.
