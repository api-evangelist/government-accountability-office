What GAO Found GAO has made 139 recommendations to the Director of National Intelligence (DNI) from July 2011 through September 15, 2026. These recommendations address issues with intelligence enterprise management, infrastructure and facilities, workforce management, and personnel vetting. As of September 15, 2026, the Office of the DNI (ODNI) has implemented 76 recommendations, or 55 percent.
