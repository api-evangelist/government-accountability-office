# U.S. Government Accountability Office (GAO)

> The Government Accountability Office is the United States government's supreme audit
> institution — the "congressional watchdog." It audits, evaluates and investigates federal
> programs for Congress and publishes the results as reports, testimonies, legal decisions and
> bid-protest rulings. **GAO publishes no public REST API, no OpenAPI/GraphQL/AsyncAPI contract,
> no SDKs and no developer portal.** Its machine-readable public surface is a catalog of RSS 2.0
> feeds plus a set of human-facing search databases. This file is an API Evangelist-generated map
> of that surface; it is not published by GAO.

## What is machine-readable

All feeds below are RSS 2.0 over HTTPS, unauthenticated, and were verified returning HTTP 200 on
2026-09-12. They are the only machine-readable endpoints GAO publishes.

- [GAO Reports](https://www.gao.gov/rss/reports.xml): Summaries of new GAO reports and testimonies.
- [GAO Reports-Brief](https://www.gao.gov/rss/reports_450.xml): Shorter summaries of the same products (redirects to /rss/reports-450.xml).
- [GAO Legal Products](https://www.gao.gov/rss/reportslegal.xml): Comptroller General decisions and opinions, including bid protests.
- [GAO Legal Products - Major Rules](https://www.gao.gov/rss/reports_majrule.xml): Congressional Review Act reports on major federal agency rules.
- [GAO Press Releases](https://www.gao.gov/rss/press.xml): Press releases from the Office of Public Affairs.
- [GAO WatchBlog](https://www.gao.gov/blog/feed): GAO's blog feed.
- [Watchdog Report Podcast](https://www.gao.gov/podcast/watchdog.xml): Podcast RSS feed.
- [Technology Assessment Products](https://www.gao.gov/rss/docsearch/featured/technology_assessment_products): Featured-search feed of GAO technology assessments and Science & Tech Spotlights.

## Search databases (human interfaces, no API)

These are searchable web applications. They have no documented JSON endpoint, no export API, and
`robots.txt` disallows crawling their query strings — query them in a browser, not with an agent.

- [Reports & Testimonies](https://www.gao.gov/reports-testimonies): The full product catalog.
- [Recommendations Database](https://www.gao.gov/reports-testimonies/recommendations-database): Open and closed GAO recommendations to federal agencies.
- [Bid Protest Search & Docket](https://www.gao.gov/legal/bid-protests/search): Bid protest decisions and the docket, updated once a day.
- [Appropriations Law Decisions](https://www.gao.gov/legal/appropriations-law/search): Comptroller General appropriations law decisions.
- [Congressional Review Act Rules Database](https://www.gao.gov/legal/congressional-review-act/search-database-of-rules)
- [Federal Vacancies Search](https://www.gao.gov/legal/federal-vacancies-reform-act/federal-vacancies-search)

## Policy and access

- [Copyright & Terms of Use](https://www.gao.gov/copyright): GAO products are U.S. government works, generally in the public domain.
- [Privacy Policy](https://www.gao.gov/privacy-policy)
- [robots.txt](https://www.gao.gov/robots.txt): `Crawl-delay: 420` for all user agents; `bytespider` and `perplexitybot` are disallowed site-wide; search, `/reports-testimonies`, `/agencies`, and every database query string are disallowed.
- [Contact Us](https://www.gao.gov/about/contact-us)
- [Stay Connected](https://www.gao.gov/about/stay-connected): GAO's own index of the feeds above.

## Notes for agents

- The edge (Akamai Bot Manager) serves a JavaScript interstitial and HTTP 403 to plain crawlers on
  HTML paths. Feed paths (`/rss/*`, `/blog/feed`, `/podcast/*.xml`) and `/robots.txt` are served
  directly. Respect the 420-second crawl delay and the robots.txt disallow list.
- No `/.well-known/` document of any kind is served: security.txt, api-catalog, openid-configuration,
  oauth-authorization-server, agent-card.json and apis.json all return 404 (see
  `well-known/government-accountability-office-well-known.yml` for the full probe record).
- Full text of GAO reports is also redistributed by GPO's govinfo, which does publish an API — that
  is a different organization's contract, not GAO's.
